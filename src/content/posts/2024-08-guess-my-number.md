**Game Rules: “Guess My Number!”** 🎯

Your goal is to guess a **secret number** between **1 and 20**. Enter your guess and click **“Check!”**:

-   If correct, you win! 🎉
-   If wrong, the game will tell you if the number is **too high** or **too low**.
-   Each wrong guess reduces your score. If your score reaches **0**, you lose.

Click **“Again!”** to restart and try to beat your high score! 🔥
